| Study programs     | partial | Story 009: `slot-types` and `programs` (7-day week template, ≤5 slots a day, level order, unique slug, delete guard) editable by the owner in `/admin`; students read slot types and published programs only; dev seed of four slot types and a draft B1→B2 program. Story 010: owner-only `task-pool` (level, title, ru/en text, slot type) and `program-plan-items` (week × day × order ≤3, level within the program, unique place, delete/shorten guards) with a week × day grid and «copy a week» above the list in `/admin`. Story 012: `enrollments` (placement result in each test's own scale, one assigned/active/paused program per student, owner-only note) assigned from the student's page in `/admin` with an email; the student sees the program on `/study` and presses «Начать» (server-side start date in her time zone). Story 018: `student-assignments` — assigning copies the program's default plan with the pool text into the student's own plan in the same transaction; Murad edits it per student from «План ученика» on her page (marked «из пула» / «своё»); the student reads only her own plan, every week, on `/study/weeks`. Story 013: `/study` shows «Сегодня» (program day, progress numbers, the day's slots and personal tasks, the 7-day grid of the current week with ✓ ½ ✕ ‖ — markers; `?day=N` opens another day read-only); `/study/weeks` lists every week with done/total and shows a week's slots and tasks; a program past its last day is closed (`finished`) on the next visit. Story 014: `slot-logs` (one row per enrollment × date × slot; students read their own, never write — every write is a Server Action that derives the enrollment, date and minutes on the server) and a «Старт»/«Стоп» timer on today's slots: `timerStartedAt` lives on the server (D-SP-4), one timer at a time, capped at 240 minutes, a slot is done only at its minimum (D-SP-7); a Web Audio signal + vibration + visual banner at the minimum; the day states and totals of 013 now read the logs; `slot_completed` analytics. Story 016: `day-comments` (one private comment per enrollment × day, ≤1000 characters; a student reads only her own and writes through a Server Action that derives the enrollment and refuses future days; empty text deletes) shown under today and past days of the current week on `/study`; Murad reads «Комментарии учеников» in `/admin` (student, program, program day, date, text; newest first; filter by student); no analytics carry the text. Story 015: «Отметить вручную» on today's and past days of the current week (Server Action `markSlotAction`: minutes replace the total, 0 resets, ≤ 600, future days refused, a timer on that slot is stopped; `slot_completed` with `viaTimer: false`; no migration). Story 017: the student pauses («Пауза», with a confirmation) and resumes («Продолжить») her own program through Server Actions that write with a server-only context (students have no write access to `status` / `pauses`); `programDay` and everything built on it (overview, timer, marking, comments) subtract paused days; paused calendar days show ‖ in the week grid and cannot be marked or commented on; a paused program is never auto-finished; a running timer is stopped on pause; `program_paused` / `program_resumed` analytics (slug only); no limit on pause length or count. Story 019: `/study/students` (owner only; anonymous goes to the admin sign-in, a student sees «not found») lists every student with day X of N, days done and missed, minutes and the last study day; `/study/students/<id>` shows her this and the previous week in the same markers (read-only), and her latest comments with their program day; nothing is written, the placement note is never loaded. Demo: `pnpm seed:study` fills a published B1→B2 program, a task pool and three students (assigned, mid-program with history and comments, paused); it runs on Vercel preview builds only (`VERCEL_ENV=preview`), never on production. Migration `enrollment_one_open` adds a partial unique index so a student cannot hold two assigned/active/paused enrollments even under a race |
